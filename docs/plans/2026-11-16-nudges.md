# Nudges: point students to Notebook Companion, and check the nudge system (Nov 16, 2026)

The teacher asked for nudges that send students to the new Notebook Companion page, and
for a look at whether the nudge system as a whole is working.

Nothing here is built. The findings come from reading `js/core/nudge.js` and where
`js/core/app.js` calls it. No student data was looked at.

## How nudges work today

There are three kinds:

| Kind | When | Where in code |
|---|---|---|
| **What to Do Next** cards (up to 2 or 3) | On the unit home screen | `getSuggestions`, `renderSuggestions` |
| **Opening toasts** (at most 2 per page load) | When a student opens an activity | `checkSmartNudge` |
| **Leaving toasts** (at most 3 per page load) | When a student goes back to Home | `onActivityComplete` |

What to Do Next picks, in order: an uncapped activity if the last one hit its daily point
cap; the next unread chapter; a review activity if any term has been missed twice; the next
untried step of Textbook > Flashcards > Fill in the Blank > Typing > Practice Test; an
activity unused for 3+ days; any untried activity.

Opening toasts: open a game before doing Learn Mode today and you are told Learn Mode earns
1.5x to 2x points; open a game with no short answer written and you are asked to write one;
play a simple game twice and you are pointed to a 1.25x game. Leaving toasts suggest a
different activity, a review activity, the next study step, or an activity unused for a while.

## Is it working? What the code shows

**1. Nothing records whether a nudge was seen or followed.** No nudge is logged, so there
is no way to tell, from data, whether any nudge changes what students do. That is the
first thing to fix if we want an answer instead of a guess.

**2. What to Do Next goes stale.** The cards are drawn once, the first time Home appears
after the page loads (`home-cards` is filled only while it is empty). A student who reads a
chapter or masters terms and comes back to Home still sees the old suggestions until they
reload, so "Read Chapter 1" can sit there after they read it.

**3. "Last used" dates are only saved on the way back to Home.** `onActivityComplete` runs
only from `showHome`. A student who goes from one activity straight to another through the
tabs never gets a "last used" date, so "you haven't tried X in N days" can be wrong.

**4. Opening toasts vanish too fast to read.** They use the default 3 seconds. The short
answer toast is about 150 characters, which most 13-year-olds cannot read in 3 seconds,
and it appears just as the game is starting.

**5. The same toast every day.** The Learn Mode toast fires the first time a student opens
any game on any day they have not done Learn Mode, at most twice a page load, every day.
Toasts that repeat this way are easy to stop reading.

**6. Most nudges sell points, not learning.** Six of the nine messages talk about points
or multipliers. That suits leaderboard-minded students and probably does little for the
rest.

**7. One function is never called.** `checkPostGameNudge` ("Nice game! Learn Mode earns
more points") has no caller.

**8. Weak terms send students to Typing Practice first.** The review pick is the first of
Typing Practice, Fill in the Blank, Flashcards that the student has tried, so a student who
once tried Typing Practice is always sent there for missed terms, whichever terms they are.

**9. Nothing points to the notebook or the quiz.** The quiz date and the reread schedule
are not part of any nudge except the new "Reread today" note.

## Proposed: nudges to Notebook Companion

| # | Nudge | Where | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **What to Do Next card** "Get your notebook ready" with a reason ("2 sections to reread, 3 missed questions"). Shown when a reread is due, when there are missed questions, or always in the last 7 days before the quiz. Top of the list in quiz week | Home | Small |
| B | **After missing questions:** leaving Practice Test or Learn Mode with new misses, a toast "The questions you missed are on your Notebook Companion list" | Leaving toast | Small |
| C | **After finishing a chapter:** marking the last section of a chapter read shows "Glue in this chapter's vocabulary half-sheet and tick it off in Notebook Companion" with a link | Textbook | Small |
| D | **Quiz week:** in the last 3 days, the "Reread today" note becomes "Quiz in 3 days: check your notebook" even if no reread is due | Home header | Small |

## Proposed: fixes to the nudge system

| # | Fix | Size |
|---|---|---|
| E | **Log nudges.** For each nudge, count times shown and times followed per student (synced, with a merge rule that keeps the higher count), and add a small "Nudges" table to the dashboard's Overview: shown, followed, rate | Medium |
| F | Redraw What to Do Next every time Home appears | Small |
| G | Save "last used" when an activity opens, not only when returning Home | Small |
| H | Opening toasts stay up long enough to read (about 1 second per 15 characters, at least 5 seconds) | Small |
| I | Each toast at most once a day per student, not every page load | Small |
| J | Rewrite toasts to lead with the learning reason, keeping points as a second line (teacher approves wording) | Small |
| K | Remove `checkPostGameNudge` | Small |
| L | Send missed terms to Flashcards first, which reviews the exact terms missed | Small |

E is what answers "is it working". With E live for two or three weeks, the dashboard
would show which nudges students follow, and the rest can be cut or reworded on evidence.

## Decisions for the teacher

1. Which notebook nudges (A to D)?
2. Log nudges and show the rates on the dashboard (E)?
3. Which fixes (F to L)?
4. Toast tone: lead with learning, keep points, or both?
5. Fewer nudges overall? For example, at most one toast per activity open.

## Decisions (Nov 16)

All of A to L, yes. Tone: lead with learning, points as a second line. At most one toast
per activity open or close.

## Built (Nov 16)

Two commits on the `nudges` branch:

1. **Nudges** (8.55.133): every nudge is logged in `nudge_log` (`tools/nudge-core.js`,
   with a merge rule); each toast is shown at most once a day, one per open or close, stays
   up about a second per 15 characters (5 to 15 s), and has an **Open** button; the toasts
   are reworded; What to Do Next is redrawn each time Home opens; "last used" is saved on
   opening; the unused `checkPostGameNudge` is gone; missed terms go to Flashcards first;
   notebook nudges A to D. The privacy page now lists read dates, the notebook checklist
   and the nudge log.
2. **Dashboard** (8.55.134): Overview > Are nudges working?, from `NudgeCore.summarize`.

### Toast wording for the teacher to approve

`Name,` is the student's first name; without one the sentence starts with a capital.

| Nudge | When | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| learn-first | Opening a game before Learn Mode today | Name, start with Learn Mode. It brings back the terms you are still learning, so the games after it stick better. / Today it also earns 1.5x points (day 2 of your streak). |
| short-answer | Opening a game with no short answer written | Name, try writing a short answer. Explaining an idea in your own words is one of the best ways to remember it. / Each finished answer earns study points. |
| harder-game | A simple game opened twice | Name, ready for a harder kind of practice? Sort It Out makes you use the terms, not just recognize them. / It earns 1.25x points. |
| after-learn | Finishing Learn Mode | Name, want to test what you just learned? Try Who Am I? while it is fresh. |
| notebook-missed | Leaving Practice Test, Learn Mode or Tower Defense with new misses | Name, the questions you missed are on your Notebook Companion list. Copy them into your notebook with the right answers. |
| mix-it-up | Same activity twice in a row | Name, switching activities helps you remember more than repeating one. Try Flashcards next. |
| review-missed | Terms missed more than once | Name, you have missed a few terms more than once. Flashcards goes over exactly those terms. |
| next-step | Next untried study step | Name, nice work! Next step in your study plan: Practice Test. |
| come-back | An activity unused 3+ days | Name, it has been 4 days since you tried Timeline. Coming back to it after a break helps it stick. |
| notebook-chapter | Last section of a chapter marked read | Name, chapter done! Glue in its vocabulary half-sheet, then tick the terms off in Notebook Companion. |

The "/" marks a line break. Home-screen note in the last 3 days: "Quiz in 2 days: check
your notebook". Notebook card: "Get your notebook ready", with a reason such as
"2 sections to reread, 1 missed question to copy" or "Quiz in 3 days: check your notebook".
