# Unit 3 fact check (Nov 14, 2026)

The teacher asked for a full accuracy check of Unit 3 before it opens on Monday, Nov 16, after an audit turned up four small errors (fixed in 8.55.126).

## Method

Five checkers, one per chapter, read every reading at all four levels, each Read More and its check, every primary-source quotation and its "What it means" note, and the chapter's vocabulary, practice questions, fill-in-the-blanks, short-answer questions (rubric, plan, example answer) and typing passages. They verified claims against primary sources (Madison's notes and the Constitution on the Avalon Project, Founders Online, treaty texts from the Governor's Office of Indian Affairs) and reputable secondary sources. Findings I was unsure of were re-checked (Richards on Shays' rebels and debt; Native American citizenship before 1924).

Checked and correct: all 60 practice-question answer keys, about 50 quotations, the dates and votes in every chapter, and the historians described in the Deep readings. Not checked: the Go Deeper pages themselves.

## Decisions (Nov 15)

- Findings 1–20 and 22–25: fix as suggested. Shipped in 8.55.128.
- Finding 21 (about two-thirds of Native Americans were citizens before 1924): leave as is.
- The five borderline items listed on the decisions page as "leave" stay as they are (no objections).

While applying the fixes:

- **Judicial review:** a Power Sort explanation repeated the "first used in 1803" wording, so it got the same fix.
- **Point Elliott:** "Lummi" is new to the text, so it got a pronunciation guide (LUM-ee) for the teacher to check.
- **"We the People":** the main readings at Quick, Clear and Full still say Morris "began it with three words: 'We the People.'" That is literally true of the final text and was outside the approved fix, so it is unchanged. The Read More and Deep now explain that the earlier draft already began "We the people of the States of…".
