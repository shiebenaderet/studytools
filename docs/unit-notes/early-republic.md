## Reading levels

Measured with `study-tools/engine/tools/check-levels.js` (Flesch–Kincaid; limits in
`docs/READING-LEVELS.md`). Whole tiers: Clear 6.1, Full 8.9, Deep 16.4. All pass.

- **Deep is very hard.** It only has to measure above Full, and it does, but it
  averages grade 16.4. The two summaries are the hardest:
  `s2-05-summary` 21.2 and `s3-06-summary` 21.3. Also above 18: `s1-05-whiskey-rebellion`
  18.2, `s2-04-election-1800` 18.5, `s3-05-trouble-abroad` 18.4. Colonial Unrest's and
  Civil War's Deep readings sit around 10.5 to 12.5. Rewriting these for length, not
  content, would bring Deep in line with the other units.
- **Some Full sections are on the easy side** of the Full target (about 8 to 9.5):
  `s3-01-jefferson-in-power` 7.1, `s3-04-lewis-and-clark` 7.1, `s1-01-no-playbook` 7.6
  (unchanged in the re-level), `s3-03-louisiana-purchase` 7.7. They still measure above
  Clear. Fine to leave; worth a look if Full feels thin.
