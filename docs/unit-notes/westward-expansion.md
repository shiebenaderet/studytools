## Check before students see it

- **Likely error: Polk and Oregon.** Full, "Polk and the Push to the Pacific" says
  Polk settled Oregon because he "needed his southern flank secure." The Oregon
  compromise (1846) secured his *northern* border with Britain while he turned south
  toward Mexico. The re-level kept the wording because it doesn't change facts; fix it
  here. Check the Clear and Deep versions of the same section for the same claim.
- **Typo fixed, worth a glance.** Clear, "The Fight for Women's Rights" had a broken
  sentence ("...end slavery completely. reminding people that Black women faced double
  the discrimination"). It now reads "She reminded people that Black women were treated
  unfairly twice as much." Confirm the meaning is what you want.
