Why three?  Option A keeps shipping risk minimal — same toolbar, polished, with new Canvas controls as another row. Option B (recommended) separates what to export from where it goes, giving Canvas's heavier settings a proper card without crowding the toolbar. Option C trades flexibility for clarity: one decision per screen, hard to get lost. The implementation cost rises left to right.
