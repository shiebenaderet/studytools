## Flashcard pictures (vocab/)

Downloaded Nov 13 2026 from Wikimedia Commons, 500 px wide. Each file's Commons record marks it public domain; the license was read from that record, not checked further. The teacher reviewed the set the same day.

- `northwest-ordinance.jpg` (Northwest Ordinance): Map of the Old Northwest Territory before and after the Ordinance of 1787, printed in 1936 (Northern Trust Co., Chicago). Commons: The_Old_Northwest_Territory_preceding_and_following_the_Ordinance_of_1787.jpg. Public domain.
- `delegate.jpg` (delegate): Howard Chandler Christy, Scene at the Signing of the Constitution of the United States, 1940 (U.S. Capitol). Commons: Scene_at_the_Signing_of_the_Constitution_of_the_United_States.jpg. Public domain.
- `legislature.jpg` (legislature): Samuel F. B. Morse, The House of Representatives, 1822 (National Gallery of Art). Commons: Samuel_Finley_Breese_Morse_-_The_House_of_Representatives_(1822).jpg. Public domain.
- `new-jersey-plan.jpg` (New Jersey Plan): William Paterson of New Jersey, who presented the plan; a later copy of a 1794 portrait by James Sharples. Commons: William_Paterson_copy.jpg. Public domain.
- `legislative.jpg` (legislative): William Birch, the Capitol in Washington, 1800 (Library of Congress). Commons: A_view_of_the_Capitol_of_Washington_before_it_was_burnt_down_by_the_British_LCCN2004661947.jpg. Public domain.
- `executive.jpg` (executive): The White House in 1846, daguerreotype by John Plumbe (Library of Congress). Commons: White_House_1846.jpg. Public domain.
- `judicial.jpg` (judicial): The Old Supreme Court Chamber in the Capitol, where the Court met from 1810 to 1860 (Architect of the Capitol). Commons: OldSupremeCourt.jpg. Public domain.
- `veto.jpg` (veto): "King Andrew the First," an 1832 cartoon attacking President Andrew Jackson for his vetoes; he holds one in his hand. Commons: King_Andrew_the_First_(political_cartoon_of_President_Andrew_Jackson).jpg. Public domain.
- `impeachment.jpg` (impeachment): The Senate trying President Andrew Johnson in 1868, drawn by Theodore R. Davis for Harper's Weekly. Commons: Andrew_Johnson_impeachment_trial.jpg. Public domain.
- `federalist.jpg` (Federalist): Alexander Hamilton, a leading Federalist, painted by John Trumbull about 1806. Commons: Alexander_Hamilton_portrait_by_John_Trumbull_1806.jpg. Public domain.
- `anti-federalist.jpg` (Anti-Federalist): Patrick Henry, a leading Anti-Federalist; portrait by George Bagby Matthews after Thomas Sully (U.S. Senate). Commons: Patrick_henry.JPG. Public domain.
- `freedom-of-speech.jpg` (freedom of speech): Women picketing the White House for the right to vote, January 1917. Commons: Suffragists_picketing_the_White_House.jpg. Public domain.
- `reservation.jpg` (reservation): Map of Indian reservations in the United States, Office of Indian Affairs, 1892 (Library of Congress). Commons: Map_showing_Indian_reservations_within_the_limits_of_the_United_States,_1892.jpg. Public domain.
- `cede.jpg` (cede): The Treaty of Greenville, painted in 1795 by an unidentified artist (Chicago History Museum). Commons: Treaty_of_Greenville.jpg. Public domain.
- `supremacy-clause.jpg` (Supremacy Clause): The opening of Article VI in the handwritten Constitution, 1787 (National Archives). Cropped from the National Archives scan of the Constitution, page 4. Public domain.

- `three-fifths-compromise.jpg` (Three-Fifths Compromise): the kneeling figure and banner cropped from `am-i-not-a-man.jpg`, an 1837 American antislavery broadside whose image first appeared on a 1787 English medallion (Library of Congress). Public domain.
