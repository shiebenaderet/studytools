Privacy & Your Data

This study tool is designed with student privacy as a priority. Here's exactly what happens with your data.

How Your Data is Stored

By default, all your study progress is saved directly in your browser using localStorage. This means:

  • Your data stays on your device
  • Nothing is sent to any server unless cloud sync is enabled
  • Clearing your browser data will reset your progress
  • Your data is specific to the browser and device you're using
If you use the site without entering your name (guest mode), no data leaves your device at all.

Cloud Sync (Optional)

If your teacher has enabled cloud sync (via Supabase), and you enter your name and period, the following data is synced:

Data Why It's Collected
Your first name and last initial To identify you on the leaderboard and for your teacher
Your class period To group students by class for the leaderboard and dashboard
A recovery word So you can get your progress back on another device. Only a scrambled version is stored, not the word itself.
Activity progress Flashcard mastery, test scores, activity completion, textbook sections read, your highlights, and reflections you write in Learn Mode
Written answers Answers you choose to send to your teacher from Short Answer, Response Builder and Read More questions
Study time Session length, total time, and the time spent in each activity on each day (kept for six weeks), for progress tracking
Leaderboard scores Composite score for class rankings (requires teacher approval to appear)
Feedback you send Bug reports and suggestions, with your browser type and screen size so a problem can be reproduced

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No student email addresses or passwords. You sign in with your name and class period.
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or fingerprinting
  • No cookies for tracking or advertising
  • No third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no tracking pixels)
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone outside your classroom

Third-Party Connections

All fonts, icons, and scripts are self-hosted. This site does not make requests to Google, Facebook, or any external analytics or advertising service. No student data is transmitted to third parties. (One exception: the Tower Defense game in older units loads its 3D library from a public content delivery network, which sees the request but no student data.)

Who Can See Your Data

  • You can see your own progress on the site
  • Your teacher can see student names, study time, progress, written answers and feedback via a private dashboard
  • Your classmates can see names and scores on the leaderboard (only after teacher approval). They never see your written answers.
  • No one else is given access. Written answers and feedback can be read only by the teacher, and only the teacher can delete records or approve leaderboard entries. Names, class periods, progress and scores work differently: students have no passwords, so the database cannot tell one student's browser from another's. A person with technical skill could read or change those records. Closing that gap needs individual sign-ins, which this site does not have yet.

Deleting Your Data

The Reset My Progress button in Edit Profile clears your progress for a unit, on your device and in the cloud. Clearing your browser's site data removes what is stored on that device. Written answers you have sent stay until your teacher removes them. To have everything about you removed, ask your teacher.

COPPA & FERPA Compliance

This tool is designed for use in K-12 classrooms. We take student privacy seriously:

  • COPPA: This site asks for a student's first name, last initial and class period. It does not ask for a full name, address, email or phone number. When cloud sync is enabled in a school setting, the teacher or school acts as the consenting party on behalf of parents under the COPPA school exception. Data is used solely for educational purposes.
  • FERPA: Student progress data (scores, study time, written answers) is classified as education records when linked to a student name. Access is intended for the student and their teacher (see "Who Can See Your Data"). No data is shared with third parties.
  • Data minimization: We collect only what is needed for the tool to function. No unnecessary personal information is gathered.

Data Retention

Local data (in your browser) persists until you clear your browser's site data. Cloud-synced data is retained for the duration of the school year. Teachers can delete individual student records at any time via the teacher dashboard.

Open Source

This project is open source. Anyone can review exactly how data is handled by reading the code on GitHub. There is nothing hidden.

Questions?

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how student data is handled, please contact your child's teacher or open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
